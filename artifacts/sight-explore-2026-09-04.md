## The two-frame reading

The owner's framing: there is the thing that says **where it fits in**,
and the thing inside that says **what it is**. Outer frame and inner
glyph. Most of the below hangs off that split.

- `explored` **Outer shape is borrowed, never invented.** BPMN,
  standard flowchart forms, internationally understood vocabulary. The
  owner is explicit that he will not create new shapes.
- `explored` **Shape carries type.** Noun subclasses get distinct
  shapes — personas, processes, tools.
- `explored` **Shape also carries firmness.** Squared forms read as
  settled and ordered; cloud forms read as unstructured, still being
  built or brainstormed. Firmness is legible from silhouette before
  anything is read.
- `explored` **Silhouette separates human from bot.** Rounded reads
  human, boxy reads machine. The owner attributes this technique to
  the film *Up*'s character design. That attribution is unverified.
  Do not file it as sourced.
- `explored` **Inner glyph is swappable skin.** Structure fixed, skin
  the user's. A persona may be drawn as a man, a woman, a dragon, an
  animal — the slot in the grammar does not change. Same pattern as
  the already-ruled swappable accents, applied to marks.
- `explored` **Fill carries state.** Distinct from shape and from
  skin.
- `explored` **Weight is an unassigned channel.** Ghosted, subdued,
  normal, bold, outlined, double-outlined, shadowed. Roughly three to
  four usable steps. The owner deliberately declined to assign it a
  meaning — logged as an available channel, not as a rule. Candidate
  meanings raised and not chosen: certainty (ghost = planned, solid =
  actual) or importance.

## 4.1 — the field

- `explored` Black is not a background colour; it is the absence of
  content. Anything drawn is asserting something is there. Nothing is
  drawn to fill space, frame, or decorate. Rationale ties to pillar
  one: the screen only holds things for you if everything on it means
  something.

## 4.6 — the null tile, and the shell

- `explored` Black means *nothing exists*. A null tile means
  *something should exist here and does not*. Absence that matters
  gets drawn.
- `explored` Two null conditions, carried as fill on one tile rather
  than as two glyphs: **never filled**, and **was filled, now broken**.
- `explored` **Broken is hazard**, using the existing field-eight
  modifier mechanism. Hazard is one of the three accents and is
  reserved — that colour means something is wrong, and nothing else
  may use it.
- `explored` **Containers report their own state, so holes are not
  drawn wholesale.** Three shell states: empty, part-filled, done.
  Individual null tiles appear only inside a part-filled container,
  where the gap is informative. A completed shell says *not your
  problem* — pillar one again. This replaced an earlier idea of a
  numeric threshold; no threshold is needed.

## 4.4 — glyph classes

- `explored` Two classes hold (nouns, imperatives — field eight
  unrevised). **Three noun subclasses:** personas, processes, tools.
  Additive, not a reversal.
- `explored` Machine value candidate: five type identifiers.

## The manifest reading

- `explored` Portal chamber signage reads as a **manifest of empty
  shells that get populated** — the sign states what the room contains
  and what you will need, as a slot list before it is a picture. A
  process step reads the same way: the shell declares the slots
  (a person, a tool, an input) and they fill as things arrive.
- `explored` This gives **planned versus actual (4.12) for free** — an
  unfilled slot is a visible hole.

## Interface shape (not bible content)

Recorded so it is not lost. Product behaviour, not sense rules. Does
not belong in the bible as written.

- Left edge carries three rails matching the three noun subclasses: a
  contacts book (personas), a binder or rolled blueprint of operations
  and plans (processes), and a shelf (tools). Each expands on
  approach.
- Tools rail is where MCP additions and user-built capabilities live.
- **Rails are summoned, not present.** Nothing exists until the owner
  has talked it into existence — progressive elaboration. This is what
  resolves the tension with the already-ruled *no shelf, no menu, no
  text box* stance: there is nothing to hide behind if nothing is
  there until you speak. The old ruling is **not** overturned.
